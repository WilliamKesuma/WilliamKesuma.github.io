Python · Time Series · Research

Modeling & Forecasting NASDAQ-100 Stock Prices with ARIMA & SARIMA

Undergraduate thesis comparing two forecasting models across five NASDAQ-100 tech stocks to identify which approach holds up best for short-term price prediction.

Investors and analysts need forecasting models they can trust, but model choice is often made on convention rather than evidence. I benchmarked ARIMA against SARIMA on four years of daily data for AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, and AMZN, testing for seasonality and comparing accuracy - all model runs landed under 2% MAPE, rated "Highly Accurate" by the Lewis (1982) standard.

  • Benchmarked across 5 NASDAQ-100 tech stocks, 4 years of daily data
  • All models under 2% MAPE ("Highly Accurate" per Lewis 1982)
  • ARIMA preferred for parsimony given no significant weekly seasonality
  • Defended and passed with revisions
